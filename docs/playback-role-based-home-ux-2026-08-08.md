# Ultimate Playback Role-Based Home UX

Date: August 8, 2026

## Decision

Ultimate Playback now keeps two home experiences:

- Account owner, Admin, Worship Leader, and Music Director keep the full command dashboard.
- Lead Singer / Setlist Creator keeps planning access.
- Regular vocals, musicians, and volunteers get a simplified member home.

## Member Home

The member home prioritizes:

- next assigned service
- setlist
- practice
- assignment response
- messages
- song suggestions
- verse of the day

The member home hides the heavier command surfaces:

- Admin panel card
- leadership stats row
- CineStage Brain status card
- Service Command Center
- dense quick action grid

## Product Rule

Playback should feel calm for serving team members. Leadership can keep operational density because they need control, planning, review, and exception handling. Regular members need one or two taps to reach their part.
