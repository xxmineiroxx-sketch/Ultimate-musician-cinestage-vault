# Competitive Benchmark: Chord Charts / Lyrics / Setlists (as of Aug 2026)

## 1. PRODUCT SNAPSHOTS

**forScore** — PDF sheet-music reader. iOS/iPadOS/macOS/visionOS only (universal purchase). **$24.99 one-time** + optional forScore Pro **$14.99/yr** (30-day pass $2.99; family-sharing Pro tier added Apr 2025). PDF-only (no native ChordPro rendering/transpose). Core: best-in-class Apple Pencil annotation (layers, stamps), setlists, half-page turns, autoscroll via audio-track recording, Bluetooth pedals (AirTurn partner since 2010), MIDI, iCloud sync, linked audio tracks with pitch/loop, Reflow teleprompter view on iPhone. 2024–26: forScore 15 (Sep 15, 2025) — Liquid Glass redesign, grid view, Control Change MIDI, tempo metadata; 14.4 (Apr 2025) — half-page-turn indicators, Shortcuts actions; 15.1 (Mar 24, 2026) — Presto Music content provider, **Genki Wave gesture-ring** integration. Companion app **Cue**: leader wirelessly turns pages on up to 15 devices, remote screen monitoring, dual-page mode. 1M copies sold; #3 top-paid US iPad app 2025. App Store 4.8★ (42K).

**OnSong 2026** — chord-chart/setlist platform, worship-leaning. iOS/Mac/visionOS; free tier (30 songs). **Essentials $3.99/mo or $29.99/yr; Premium $5.99/mo or $59.99/yr** (annual major-version rebrand; v2026.1 shipped Jan 19, 2026). Core: ChordPro/PDF/Word import, transpose/capo/diagrams, autoscroll, foot pedals, annotation, backing/click/multitracks, MIDI automation + lighting scenes, lyrics projection (HDMI/AppleTV/Google Cast), Connect/OnCue real-time team sync, Console web library manager, SongSelect/Planning Center/Dropbox import. **OnSong 2026 additions: ChordFlow™ on-device PDF chord detection + transposition, Key Finder (sing bars → suggested key, patent-pending), mic-based Tempo Detection, Voice Control, TalkThrough™ AI-voice onboarding, built-in tuner, OnSong® Charts content service, Versions, Coda Stomp Mini pedal.** App Store 4.5★ (5.4K).

**Ultimate Guitar (app)** — tab/chord catalog, not a gig tool. iOS/Android/Vision; free + **Pro $9.99/mo; bundle (Pro + Courses + Sing) $59.99/yr** (IAP slots show annual Pro at $29.99–$39.99; web pricing differs, see pain points). Core: 1M+ crowd-sourced tabs/chords/lyrics, interactive Pro tabs, 29K+ HQ tabs w/ backing tracks + synchronized lyrics, autoscroll, transpose, metronome, tuner, Tonebridge effects, offline favorites, dark mode "for gigs," Personal tabs, left-handed mode. Recent: Shots short-video, UG Courses (2,500+ lessons), UG Sing karaoke. No setlists (playlists only), no band sharing, no pedals. App Store 4.7★ (471K iOS).

**Planning Center Music Stand** — worship-team reader, add-on to Planning Center Services. iOS/Android; app free but requires paid Services plan + **Music Stand add-on $5–$10/mo**. Core: setlists auto-built from Services plans, PDF/chord-chart annotation (highlights/drawings/notes), page rearrange, foot-pedal + swipe, **leader-linked page turning across all band devices**, external-display mirroring, metronome w/ visual flash, audio player with section looping. App Store **3.53★ (321)** — weakest rating in this set.

**SongbookPro** — cross-platform songbook. Android/iOS/Windows/Fire. **Solo one-time ~$6–7 per platform** (iOS IAP $5.99; 12-song free trial); **Groups subscription from $4/user/yr** (Band 6 users / Ensemble 12 / Orchestra 20, +$40 per 10 extra; 21-day trial, web-only purchase). Core: ChordPro/OnSong/PDF/Word import, UG/WorshipTogether/e-chords in-app search, transpose/capo (text charts), handwritten notes w/ 20 colors + highlighter, metronome, dark theme, Bluetooth pedals, full offline, free Manager desktop app. 2024–26: **Groups launched Nov 2024** (shared libraries, web management, granular permissions); 25.1 (Autumn 2025) — synced handwritten notes (personal devices only), chromatic tuner, pinned setlists, per-song MIDI, ChordPro grid syntax; Winter 2026 — web PDF cropping, library analytics/copyright reports. App Store 4.7★ (2.6K iOS).

**BandHelper** — band-management suite w/ performance mode. iOS/Android/web; cloud subscription per band (Basic/Plus/Pro): **Solo $16–32/yr; 2–5 users $40–80/yr; 6–20 users $60–120/yr; 21–100 users $100–200/yr**. Core: auto-synced repertoire/setlists/lyrics/chords to bandmates, transpose/capo/horn keys, reference recordings w/ speed + loop controls, backing/click/video tracks, **MIDI presets driving keyboards/effects/lighting per song**, foot switches, plus scheduling/gig invites, finance, stage plots, invoices, practice log. App Store 4.27★ (112).

## 2. TABLE STAKES 2026

- ChordPro + PDF import; instant transpose/capo on text charts (all except forScore, which is PDF-only)
- Stylus-grade annotation: layers, highlighter, undo, typed notes (forScore sets the bar)
- Bluetooth pedal page turns (AirTurn/PageFlip), half-page turns, intelligent autoscroll
- Setlist building + sharing to bandmates; offline-first libraries
- Dark mode / stage readability (high-contrast chords, big fonts, visual-flash metronome)
- Linked audio/backing tracks with loop/speed; built-in tuner + metronome (both now in SongbookPro 25.1, OnSong 2026, UG)
- Cloud backup/sync across a user's devices (iCloud or proprietary)
- Lyrics teleprompter / external-display output (OnSong, Music Stand, BandHelper)
- Freemium trial tier (forScore is the lone pure paid-upfront holdout)

## 3. EMERGING / AI-FORWARD DIFFERENTIATORS

- **On-device audio/vision AI — OnSong 2026 leads the category**: ChordFlow detects chords inside PDFs for real-time transposition; Key Finder derives a singer's key from a sung phrase; mic-based tempo detection; voice-controlled hands-free operation; AI-voice guided onboarding (TalkThrough). No rival here ships comparable AI.
- **Novel hands-free inputs**: forScore Pro face-gesture page turns (head/wink/lips, face-tracking devices); forScore 15.1 native Genki Wave ring gestures; AirTurn's Power Stand (battery stand) shows pedal-ecosystem maturation.
- **Leader→follower sync as performance infra**: Music Stand linked devices, forScore Cue (15 devices + remote screen view), OnSong Connect/OnCue — role-based "bandleader drives, members follow" is now a checkbox differentiator.
- **Built-in content pipelines**: UG's catalog w/ synced-lyric backing tabs; OnSong Charts (new 2026); forScore providers (Musicnotes, Presto Music 2026); SongbookPro in-app UG/WorshipTogether search.
- **Web consoles for band admins**: SongbookPro Groups (library analytics, set-play frequency, copyright reporting — 2026), OnSong Console, BandHelper web.
- **Stage automation (MIDI/DMX)**: OnSong scenes/lighting, BandHelper per-song MIDI patches, SongbookPro per-song MIDI, forScore Control Change.
- **Apple Watch**: no confirmed current Watch remote among the six (unverified); companion-device remotes (Cue, Connect) fill the gap.

## 4. USER PAIN POINTS

- **Ultimate Guitar**: Sitejabber 1.5★/103 reviews (2024–25): free trials convert to $75–$125 annual charges, refunds refused or partial, auto-renew price hikes ($24.99→$39.99), heavy ads/upsell even for paying users, opaque strikethrough "sale" pricing ($299.99→$64.99). Classic dark-pattern subscription backlash.
- **Planning Center Music Stand**: 3.53★ (321 iOS ratings) — by far the lowest here; recurring themes are app instability and that it requires a paid Services plan; worship-only scope.
- **forScore**: iPadOS 26 broke popovers/UI at v15 launch (acknowledged in the 15.0.6 roadmap post); Oct 2025 support-staffing collapse left tickets unanswered (solo-developer risk); review-cited cons: Apple-only lock-in, no folder hierarchy, learning curve.
- **OnSong**: 2019–2020 pivot from $19.99 paid app to subscription alienated legacy users (historical, as of 2020); power features (annotation, MIDI, backing tracks, Console) gated behind Premium; 4.5★ trails forScore.
- **BandHelper**: 4.27★ (112) — utilitarian/dated UI; per-band subscription complexity.
- **SongbookPro**: platforms sold separately (iOS/Android/Windows each paid); handwritten notes deliberately don't sync to Groups bandmates; 12-song free cap.
